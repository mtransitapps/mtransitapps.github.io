MonTransit website privacy policy

This policy applies to this website. For the mobile application, see the separate mobile app privacy policy.

Optional website analytics

With your consent, we use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand website usage and improve the site. It uses cookies and collects page URLs, referrers, browser and device information, visits, engagement, and interactions such as outbound link clicks and clicks on "Use my location". Google processes network information such as your IP address to provide the service and derive approximate location; GA4 does not log or store individual IP addresses. Analytics data is not necessarily anonymous.

We do not send browser geolocation coordinates to Analytics. Firebase Performance Monitoring is not currently enabled.

Your choice

Analytics is disabled until you select "Accept". "Reject" leaves the website available without Analytics. Use the "Privacy settings" button on any tracked page to change your choice. Rejecting after acceptance stops future Analytics collection and reloads the page; it does not erase previously collected data or necessarily remove existing Analytics cookies. You can clear cookies using your browser settings.

Your choice is saved in this browser's local storage for this website. Clearing that storage prompts you to choose again. Browser location permission is separate from Analytics consent.

Map and third-party services

The homepage loads Google Maps and other external resources independently of Analytics consent. It can request approximate location from ipwho.is using your IP address, and use browser geolocation if you grant location permission, to center the map. These services receive network information needed to respond to your requests even if you reject Analytics.

Google processes Analytics data under its Privacy Policy. See also the IPWHOIS privacy policy. Data may be processed outside your country.

Retention and requests

Our GA4 user-level and event-level data retention setting is 14 months. This setting does not apply to all aggregated reports, and cookies have their own lifetimes. For website privacy questions or requests concerning your data, contact mtransit.apps+privacypolicy@gmail.com. Our ability to locate particular records depends on the information available; we do not promise that individual records can always be identified.
